Does HDR video glow on LinkedIn?

PUBLISHED 3 AUGUST 2026 · BY NIMROD YARKONI

Short answer: HDR video does not reliably glow on LinkedIn, but HDR images still do. Video pipelines on major platforms tone-map or clamp HDR brightness, a response to creators flooding short-form feeds with searing HDR clips. LinkedIn's image pipeline is the exception that makes the glow trend possible: it strips most HDR metadata from uploaded JPEGs but keeps the ICC color profile, so a correctly encoded image arrives in the feed with its glow intact. That image route is what Superwhite automates.

Why video got clamped and images didn't

HDR video abuse came first and at scale: whole clips mastered at maximum brightness, autoplaying in dark feeds. Platforms responded by tone-mapping video down to comfortable levels. Still images went through a different pipeline that nobody hardened the same way, and LinkedIn's JPEG pipeline in particular preserves color profiles through compression. That accident of infrastructure is the entire reason the glowing logo trend lives on LinkedIn and not somewhere else.

What about auto HDR on phones?

Phone cameras with auto HDR capture more dynamic range and may save Ultra HDR files with gain maps. That is a different mechanism from the LinkedIn glow: platforms mostly strip or ignore gain maps on upload, and the result rarely renders above white in the feed. The glow that survives LinkedIn is specifically a Rec.2100 PQ ICC profile inside a plain JPEG.

So what actually glows on LinkedIn today?

Feed post images, uploaded directly as JPEGs with the HDR profile embedded, not cropped or edited in the composer. That is how the Wiz logo, Port, Taboola, Lusha and DoiT did it. You can do the same in about a minute:

  1. Drop your logo into superwhite.app.
  2. Set peak brightness and the glow threshold.
  3. Download the JPEG and post it to LinkedIn as a feed image, unedited.

Why do HDR images glow on LinkedIn but videos don't?
